The best free CleanMyMac alternatives

Updated: October 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

CleanMyMac has no free tier, only a 7-day trial, then about $40 a year for one Mac. If you mainly need to clear caches, uninstall apps cleanly and get disk space back, free options cover it: Tidymint (free, open source, all-in-one cleaner), AppCleaner and Pearcleaner (uninstallers), OnyX (maintenance for power users) and macOS's own Storage settings. None of them replace CleanMyMac's malware scanner.

Skip "free" cracked copies. A cleaner asks for Full Disk Access and your admin password, and Jamf Threat Labs has documented pirated Mac apps carrying backdoors.

5 free alternatives

1. Tidymint: free, open-source all-in-one cleaner

Tidymint covers cleanup, clean-by-app pages for browsers and messengers, duplicates, large files, an uninstaller with undo, a bubble map of your disk and a menu bar indicator. Its defining rule: everything goes to the Trash, nothing is deleted permanently until you empty it. It is MIT-licensed, works offline and is available in English and Vietnamese. Built with AI and developer workflows in mind, it also knows folders like .ollama and project drafts.

2. AppCleaner: the classic uninstaller

Free. Drag an app onto its window and it lists the related files to remove. Does one thing well.

3. Pearcleaner: open-source uninstaller

Free and open source. A modern uninstaller that also finds orphaned files from apps you already removed.

4. OnyX: maintenance for power users

Free and long-running. Many cleaning and system options, but the interface assumes you know macOS well.

5. macOS Storage settings

System Settings › General › Storage offers Optimize Storage, Empty Trash Automatically and lists of large documents. Completely safe, but it doesn't touch caches or explain System Data.

FAQ

Is there a free version of CleanMyMac?

No. There is a 7-day free trial with full features, available from the MacPaw Store, the App Store and Setapp. After that you need a subscription or a one-time purchase.

Is CleanMyMac worth it?

It is a polished suite with a malware scanner and support. If you only need cleanup and uninstalling, free tools do those jobs.

Is Tidymint safe?

Tidymint only moves items to the Trash, so anything can be put back. It never touches Photos, Mail, keychains, cloud storage roots or Apple system caches, and its source code is public.

Why does macOS block Tidymint the first time?

It is not notarized by Apple yet, which requires a paid developer account. Open it once via System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway, as Apple describes.

Does Tidymint work on Intel Macs?

Yes. It runs on macOS 14 or later on both Apple silicon and Intel.
